Illustrative showcase — read this. This is ONE midlatitude band — a single β-plane latitude zone about 55° of longitude wide, in one hemisphere — NOT a planet-wide flow. The rest of the globe is left bare because the model resolves only this band. The streaming particles are illustrative: they imply persistent currents, but the instantaneous eddy heat flux is about 92% reversible — it mostly sloshes back and forth, and the genuine net transport is only the small remaining ~8% (the emergent eddy diffusivity κ). “Currents carrying heat” is an artistic reading the numbers do not validate.
